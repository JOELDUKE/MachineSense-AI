# Future Scope

## Overview

MachineSense AI has been developed as a prototype to demonstrate how IoT and AI can make predictive maintenance affordable for MSMEs. While the current system focuses on real-time monitoring and AI-based maintenance prediction, there are several opportunities to further enhance its capabilities and make it suitable for large-scale industrial deployment.

---

## Future Enhancements

### WhatsApp and Email Notifications

Integrate instant notifications through WhatsApp, email, or SMS so that maintenance teams can receive alerts immediately when abnormal machine conditions are detected.

### Mobile Application

Develop a cross-platform mobile application that allows factory owners to monitor machine health, receive alerts, and access maintenance reports from anywhere.

### Edge AI on ESP32

Deploy lightweight AI models directly on the ESP32 to perform anomaly detection locally. This will reduce cloud dependency, improve response time, and enable operation even with limited internet connectivity.

### Digital Twin

Create a digital twin of each machine to visualize its current operating condition, maintenance history, and predicted future performance in real time.

### Multiple Factory Support

Extend the platform to monitor multiple factories, production lines, and machines from a single Smart Factory Dashboard with role-based access control.

### ERP Integration

Integrate the system with Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) software to automate maintenance scheduling, inventory management, and service record updates.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Our long-term vision is to develop MachineSense AI into a complete smart factory solution that combines IoT, Artificial Intelligence, and cloud technologies to help MSMEs improve productivity, reduce maintenance costs, and accelerate their adoption of Industry 4.0 technologies.
