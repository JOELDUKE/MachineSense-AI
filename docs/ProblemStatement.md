# Problem Statement

## 1. Introduction

Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) play a crucial role in the manufacturing sector. However, many of these industries still rely on conventional industrial machines that were not designed with smart monitoring or predictive maintenance capabilities. While these machines continue to perform their intended tasks, they provide little information about their operating condition, making it difficult to identify potential issues before they result in machine failure.

In most MSMEs, maintenance is carried out only after a breakdown occurs. This reactive approach often leads to unexpected production downtime, increased repair expenses, delayed deliveries, reduced productivity, higher energy consumption, and a shorter machine lifespan. These challenges not only affect daily operations but also limit the ability of small industries to compete in an increasingly technology-driven manufacturing environment.

Although Industry 4.0 technologies have introduced advanced predictive maintenance solutions, many of these systems require significant investment in new equipment, software, and supporting infrastructure. For many MSMEs, such solutions are financially challenging to adopt, leaving a gap between traditional manufacturing practices and modern smart factory technologies.

## 2. Proposed Solution

MachineSense AI is designed to bridge this gap by providing an affordable predictive maintenance solution for existing industrial machines. Instead of replacing machinery, the proposed system enables manufacturers to upgrade their existing industrial machines into smart, connected machines using an affordable IoT retrofit kit. 

By continuously monitoring machine health and analyzing operating conditions, the system helps identify potential issues before they develop into major failures. This allows maintenance activities to be planned in advance, reducing unexpected downtime and minimizing repair costs.

The solution also provides factory owners with a clear view of machine performance through an easy-to-use dashboard, enabling informed maintenance decisions and improving overall operational efficiency.

MachineSense AI aims to make predictive maintenance practical and accessible for MSMEs, helping them improve machine reliability, reduce operational costs, increase productivity, and take a step toward Industry 4.0 without the need to replace their existing machinery.
